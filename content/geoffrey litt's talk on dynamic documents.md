[[geoffrey-litt]] gave a talk on dynamic documents at [[recurse-center]]. 

---

he speaks about a new kind of paradigm; something i've seen before in a [stanford-talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MbWgRuM-7X8); of using a dynamic-document in lieu of 'apps'. he currently works at notion, and they're trying to implement a similar thing there. 

he spoke about digging himself a big rabbit hole at rc, and then following it by talking to similar-minded people (which led him to his ph.d. at mit). 

i think the abstract idea of a new way to interact with the computer was interesting. it is, in some ways, akin to where i am heading for [[thesis/index|thesis]]. 

i think it's just to have an inkling, and to follow it courageously.
