# philosophy: 
itp academically mandates each student to maintain a blog. 

in the three years that i worked before itp, i maintained a closed  *[zettlekasten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zettelkasten)* or 'digital-brain'. the idea was to not let information blow by, and to process what i absorb via writing. 

![[z_images/250901 • obsidian-map.webp|487]]

<figcaption>a screenshot of my older <em>zettlekasten</em>.</figcaption>

the chance to make an itp-blog was to do what i did before, but better. 

in my first year, i also realized the importance of writing (and sharing) in public. i would have random conversations with people, just because they read a thought that i posted on this blog; or see someone figure out an electronic-component by going through my experiments-log. 

---
# system:
the blog is written in a rhizomatic fashion [^1] — meaning that everything is a note. conversations, thoughts, reading-notes, lectures, observations, et-cetera; are all stored, as much as possible; with equal weightage.

![[z_images/250901 • temporal-drawings(1).webp|490]]

the only structure that i am forced to include is that notes from each course are stored in their specific folders. these are typically lecture-notes and a log of weekly-experiments done for the course. everything else is out in the open.

since *everything* can be a note, the constraint to write long pieces of text is removed. therefore, many shorter notes can often be interconnected with one-another, and new (synthesized) notes are born as a result. for example, a conversation with someone provides new insight into a thought i formally had,  and then that new thought is written down as a separate note.

![[z_images/250901 • temporal-drawings(2).webp|489]]

notes are still categorized, so that they are searchable by buckets, via tags. for example, search for #writings. 

often, a bunch of these interconnected notes will lead to something that i can present externally — talks, projects, pieces of writing — and that will be documented formally on my [website](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io).
