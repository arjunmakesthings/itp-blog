> genotype denotes the specific genetic constitution of an organism, comprising the set of alleles—variations of genes—inheritied from both parents. This genetic framework determines the potential traits of the organism.

> phenotype represents the physical and functional manifestation of that code. Phenotypes include observable characteristics like eye color, height, body shape, or behavioral traits.
