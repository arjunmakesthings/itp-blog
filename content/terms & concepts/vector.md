a vector is simply an arrow that contains the following: 
- direction (angle)
- magnitude (length). 

when you express it onto a cartesian-plane, it gets two vector components: 
- x-component (`x'`)
- y-component (`y'`)

how many x' and how many y' to reach tip of a vector in x, y space.
