i rarely have time to look at myself in the mirror anymore. 

recently, i caught a glimpse on the floor-bathroom. i received my first strand of white-hair. 

![[IMG_7550.webp|430]]

strange, unanticipated reminder that time is flying by;<br>
that i am wildly chasing something that i might not get;

that i am getting older & wiser;

and that soon this time may be over. 

soon, all my hair will turn white. 

people will look at me and consider me old. 

soon, i may not be a student anymore. 

soon, i may have students flocking around me. will i be considered a good teacher? 

will i look like [[tom]]? or [[shaunaq]]? 

or will i lose my hair like [[shobhan]]?
