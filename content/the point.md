a ramble. 

---

[[ryan]], [[gwen]], [[sharleen]], [[sarah rothberg]], [[allison parrish]] & [[pranjali]] pushed me to write this, by alluding to similar points in different ways. 

---

a point of a lot of this (especially with [[thesis/index|thesis]]) is why you (as the audience) should care. 

yes, ==you're forced to listen to & interact with my work== (as a cohort, and as audience of the show). it doesn't mean i've considered you as an audience member. but then what can it do for you? 

what can it do for me: 
it ==helps me connect with you==. i express things that words can't quite articulate, and ==i can feel satisfied with my expression==. here, i'm looking for: =="yes, this thing feels like this"==. 

maybe connection with me isn't that valuable. so what can it do for you? 

i wonder: were artists thinking of their audiences? was picasso thinking that this piece would do something for you? or was he exploring the form? 

i'm giving myself the medium to communicate with the world. as someone in [[alumni chats]] said: "i'm inventing my practice". 

but ok, i'll entertain the question (because i must): 

what does it do for you:
- perhaps be able to say: "yes, this is what it feels like to me too"

yeah, the boat stops at resonance. 

making for ==people who're lonely, and can't articulate how==. 

==who feel a kind of loneliness that they cannot quite put into words. their movement in space, their communication with other people. they're part of the larger system, but their connection with it is dissonant==.
