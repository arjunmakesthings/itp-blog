3-credit class taken by [[craig fahner]] & [[luke dubois]] in semester-2. i cross registered in tandon (idm department) for this class. 

---
# why: 
i got very excited with the idea of using off-the-shelf electronic components to make sound during [[intro-to-physical-computing/index|intro-to-physical-computing]]. [[tom]] then told me to reach out to [[luke dubois]], and sound-studio seemed like the class that i could explore my particular interest in. 

i am excited by the possibility of live-performing weird music (with code / electricity), something that i'd like to get into during my time in new-york.
