sitting here wondering. writing unorganized thoughts: 

---

went for a diwali-celebration. wondered how difficult it is to socialize for me, on the way back. 

---

on a train ride back home this week, i wondered about ==artistic responsibility==. people attend shows to be dissuaded away from the normalcy of their lives momentarily; volunteering in an act of suspending disbelief. what power you hold over them. ==they come to you seeking an escape, but you have the opportunity to provoke; change courses; make them think; ignite wonder.== 

do we handle this power well? are we even taught to? 

perhaps not. ==most of what we do is give physical form to the tik-tok algorithm==. in a show, people hop from table-to-table (as if scrolling) to be momentarily engaged with a project that a student spent weeks on. you get comments & likes in the form of vague comments: "this is so cool". the itp-shows are not designed to make people think — it's impossible when there are 400 projects for people to move on to (in order to avoid fomo). or perhaps that is the challenge. 

---

in a vast sea of nothingness, what gives life meaning? 

[[gwen]] once said that my work gives my life meaning. i didn't strongly agree then; i do now. ==as i grow older, i recognize how difficult it is to find likeminded people== — like [[dan-o]] said: it's quite a miracle that two vectors meet (via conversation) in this vast multidimensional space. new-york exposed me to many people — perhaps far more than i'd met ever before in a single year — but strong connections have been rare. 

or is it me? have i become so set in my ways, and lived as if under a rock, that i've become far more distant than other people in my community? 

do you have people you like spending time with? that's amazing; if you do. 

---

work is an obsession. these objects must be realized through me. will it end after itp? will i give up, like i did in 2024? will i run out of juice again? 

---

i need to spend some time quietly writing. for [[thesis/index|thesis]]. i'm afraid of saying it, but who the heck cares: i'm trying too hard to be someone i'm not. and i'm trying too hard to convince other people. i'm trying too hard to convince myself. 

the thesis is artistic; like [[mimi]] mentioned. stop denying it. to refute [[shobhan]]'s argument, just articulate the intention — which you know you have. being drawn to something is a sign of intuition; you just need to figure out why. 

don't try to be impressive. go back to [[the thesis just needs to be honest]]. wrote [[the thesis does not need to be impressive]].
