last week, an [open call](https://itp.nyu.edu/itp/were-hiring/) from itp looking for assistant professors made its way to me. for weeks, i wondered whether i should apply. i convinced myself against it for an assortment of reasons: not good enough; still a student; haven't achieved anything; absurd to apply as a student; and so on. 

today, i wake up feeling differently. i think i don't want to apply because, at the moment, ==i don't wish to teach.==

i think i want to make work; i want to gain the collective-respect of the larger community; ==gather; before i disseminate.==

---

[[tanika]] once asked me what my ideal future is. 

![[20_ideal-future.webp|437]]

i kept alluding that it's to be able to teach. that — actually — was a scared reply. that's a *safe* choice. actually, ==what i really want to do, is to produce work. i fucking want to make work.==

---

upon further reflection, i wonder if it's because of the students i've seen here. students that i've seen in classes, on their laptopns, non-verbal; or disrespectful ones; or the ones yawning; or on instagram, tik-tok, and reddit. 

i don't know. 

i'm still a victim of circumstance, and will take the path that offers adequate security. even then, good to know that i really just want to make; i am an artist.
