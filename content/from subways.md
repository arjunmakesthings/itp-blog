small collection of subway observations:

---

defeated souls;
gathered in-between; 
moving panels tightened with screws, nuts & bolts.

---

some couples anew; 
others withered;
some on an evident path to destruction.

out of love; 
out of words; 
defeated;
succumbed. 

---

first-timers; 
veterans; 
homeless;
housed. 

---

performers; 
geniuses; 
smart people;
children with ill-formed political opinions; 
smiles in one cabin;
acts of hate in another; 
wandering eyes; 
some unsafe; 
some comforting; 
looks of indifference; 
contacts of belonging. 

---

stories of love that never began; 
existed in split-seconds before the doors opened; 
before new faces; 
before new possibilities;
before the sudden death
of our trapped suspension in motion.

---

weary eyes; 
bodies;
souls; 
sucking in strange black liquids; 
to see faint slivers of life. 

---

workers; 
business-men; 
fashion-models; 
actors & actresses; 
students & ceos;
the diaspora begins their morning underground; 
while the sun shines away.

---
