something you see, can hold, and manipulate; and that which suspends your belief ([[intro-to-computational-media/a pretty definition of wonder|a pretty definition of wonder]]), or feels like [[magic]], is more powerful than what you can't see (or experience with your senses; touch being a primary one). 

i think this is heavily based on wysiati (what you see is all there is) — the tendency of people to believe what they can see (or, by extension, experience through senses).
