felt inspired after reading [[shobhan]]'s blog: https://sssl03.github.io/purdue-garden/log/26.3fa-fall/261025.-week-9

funny how inspiration works. he first inspired me to take certain decisions with this log; i then inspired him to start writing again; and now he inspires me to sit down & write again. 

i don't do that as often as i used to in my first-year here — sit down; reflect & write. i realized that i'm spread too thin now; taking up way more than i can handle. i covered [[nataly]]'s shift at the shop on friday, and that threw off my whole week. i don't go out for tno-s, and i don't socialize with any first-years. i don't play ping-pong with [[bairui]] anymore either.

---

it's been a frustrating week. [[prisha]] & i made progress on [[analog synth build]] (see [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=REOZ9q-hsw4)) — i'm happy to see it come back to life. i do think it provides value for itp students — just the other week, [[jacob]] asked me an audio-filtering question, and i gave him my high-pass-filter circuit from the wall. once we open it up to people, i genuinely believe it can prove to be of value. [[prisha]] & i need to put in a lot of work for that to happen though. 

[[electrowetting]]'s been a bust. i can feel that i'm so close, and that if i do make it, it'll be beautiful — but i don't know if i'm chasing an impossible goal. [[nikolai]] persuaded me to try for one more week. i'm close to giving up on that — i have so many more ideas to pursue. 

had somewhat difficult conversations with [[sharleen]]; simply because it's difficult to talk about the thesis without showing it. i half-persuaded myself to make & show software-simulations — to atleast exhibit what might happen once i make my physical-displays — but decided against it. it's not worth the effort. i know i need to make it physical; i just know. 

i thought a bit about romantic-connections this week. i don't think i want to write about it; or ponder, even.

---

i wondered about [[ai-psychosis]] this week. ==have you noticed how people who've offloaded everything to a.i. are more hyperactive; disconnected; averse to differing opinions; on the edge; in an echo chamber; unwilling to ponder over ideas (because will to action is so quick)?== spoke a bit with [[aram]] about this.

attempted to write proposals many times this week. failed. i want to quit all classes & just be absorbed into my [[thesis/index|thesis]]. impossible. 

was disheartened after a conversation with [[midori]] about credits for next semester. i either pay, or take a one-credit class (which i argued just isn't worth it). 

---

[[savannah]] shared a book that made me want to write letters again. seeing [[shobhan]] write & carry out his life the way he does reminds me of the person i used to be — a bit more poetic; a bit more romantic; a person who'd make the effort that people nowadays wouldn't. i miss that version of myself. 

something happened; something after itp, [[sakina]], the absorption into work — i've become cold(er) than i was. 

i cant even write well anymore. i don't pay attention anymore. i'm so absorbed — every atom of my being sucked into the work i'm doing. i'm in a state of obsession, and willing only because i'm convinced that it's temporary. i look forward to going back to the person i used to be; only, perhaps, a bit more technically astute & artistically surer.
