been pondering about this. 

we've been taught, as designers, to pose questions with the prefix: *how might we*; insinuating weakly that people, *better* than the people the thing is being made for, *might* be able to find *one* way that *might* help. 

i wonder if we'd be better off posing questions with *how can we*; asserting that people with a certain degree of confidence in their skill will *exhaust* ways to *definitively* solve the problem at hand.
