# writing: 
> ask: Listen to Barbara Tversky who studies “embodied cognition” and write a short reaction.
> 
> Barbara Tversky talks about thinking at that border of the abstract and the embodied. You are not a brain in a vat but instead use your body and the physical world to think. What practical ideas can you draw from her talk for making your interface?
> 
> She also talks about how the same cell architecture in your brain used for physical navigation is used for processing very abstract concepts. Examine your figures of speech to see how much of your most conceptual is based on physical metaphors.
