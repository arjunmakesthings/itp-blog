# making / writing: 
> ask:
> 
> The challenge is to create an app that ==allows people to find each other in the vast space of other people==.
> 
 > Could neural networks do a better job of depicting the nuance of what is inside you and relate it more productively to the stuff inside someone else? What we have is an interface problem and you are the perfect person to save us. Can you revisit that interface? Can you ==expose more the vast number of dimensions in a neural network?==
 > 
> For people to represent themselves in the space they need to be given some opportunity to express themselves. How can you ==inspire them to write, talk, make images, or just make interesting choices.==
> 
> In the first week Hoffman talked about ITP (Interface Theory of Perception) where time and space is just a simplifying interface to a deeper reality. You need to ==create an interface metaphor (like Hoffman's time and space) using something simplified and familiar== so people will understand how to navigate this vast "impossible" space.
> 
> The ==most famous interface metaphor is the desktop metaphor but you might want to inspire less business like expression==. As we have become familiar with computer interface, that has unfortunately that as devolved into no metaphor at all, every just knows how to use pulldown menus, tab menus, carousels etc. This is what vibe programming will deliver, a corporate looking interface of compute native items. ==Each of you should come up with a metaphor, the beach, the subway, a snake, the movie Groundhog Day, smoke, atoms. Many of you already have metaphors and you can just go deeper==.
> 
> If what we depicting is your inner experience (or even an outer world that might really be a multiverse without time or free will) maybe we should thinking past conventional illusions and be more adventurous in our juxtapositions. Writing does this. Cinema did this by taking liberties with the discontinuities of time and space in editing, Computational media does this natively but the interface of DOM elements like pulldown menus for filtering is not inspiring. The "web" is a more promising metaphor, maybe keep going in that direction. Be adventurous, embrace discontinuity, make the multiverse your metaphor!
> 
> Many metaphor will be about 3D space but be careful when the only way to scale it is the equivalent of urban sprawl and the only way to navigate it is tedious travel. Many VR interfaces are like this. Instead of traveling verbs, look for conjuring verbs that allow the space to re-render around you instead of requiring to move through it. The space is constructed by your consciousness and appears to be coming out of you instead of you traveling through it. This is the idea that we have talked about of navigation by creation. Again this draws inspiration from physics with the idea of many realities "superimposed" (opposite of sprawl) on each other with the possibilities "collapsing" onto one with your observation.
> 
> Conceive of and draw novel interfaces using unexpected analogies and metaphors that might expose a higher dimensional space and allow navigation and creation in it.
> 
> If you don't have another idea about why people want to find each other, make a dating app.
> 
> Watch movies, rse a pencil and paper, upload image to blog.
> 
> As a practical interface problem, we have one screen and many stories. Are we currently living through a similar problem because there are currently infinite universes that we are walking through all the time. I sounds crazy but many (most) physicist believe this. If not infinite, you can imaging that there around 8 billion universes on earth at the moment. Can you draw inspiration for you interface from the idea of superimposition, where there appears to be only one universe, one screen, one story, but different people depart in small ways from that viewpoint?
