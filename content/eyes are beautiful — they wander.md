interesting thought to explore in picture-making: 

in [[intro-to-computational-media/icm_class-10|icm_class-10]], i realised: 

> <mark>people will <em>always</em> see more things than you originally intended</mark>

this means that the eye wanders across an image — it does not stay at the focal point you intended it to stay at. maybe an image can be created as the eye wanders across it. 

what can be revealed only when the eye falls on those corners of a canvas? 

---
eyes are beautiful. they wander over surfaces, gather what they need to sense-make; and it is almost always not what the maker wanted them to gather.
