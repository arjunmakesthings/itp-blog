no intention; mere words; saturday afternoon. 

![[IMG_8298.webp|400]]

---

[[shobhan]]'s call woke me up from a slumber that i did not want to wake up from. i've been having dreams that feel vividly real; stories that i must reach the end of. 

---

i usually sleep by 0200, and wake up by 0430, only to force myself back to sleep until my next appointment. i wonder if i should just begin waking up when my eyes open, and consider it a natural alarm-signal. 

signals. the body sends signals. i'm poor at reading them. 

---

the sunlight bounces off my polished floor. eating buttered-toast reminds me of my father. 

---

i wonder about obsession & its extent. at what point is it too much? i take a day off, and i don't know what to do with my time. the guilt consumes me. the anxiety to make the most of my time here is slowly chipping away at my ability to do so. 

==when did rest become so foreign to me?==

people remind me that rest is important; that things take time — and so on — but acceptance fights a losing battle with cognition every time. 

the idea of an itp-thesis is so daunting. the idea of being a second-year student is so daunting. the future as an international immigrant is so daunting. 

---

plants grow very quickly. a couple of weeks ago, this was barren. now there are plants. i don't know much about plants. [[taksh]] would know.

---

maybe everything needs to be lower-pressure than what it is right now. nothing is so important; nothing is so consequential.

maybe the thesis *can* be an art-project. maybe the thesis *can* be something small of interest. 

[[the thesis just needs to be honest]]. 

and, maybe, with that i must come to terms with my place in the universe, and the extent of my abilities. ==maybe i need to accept that i won't produce something amazing.==

i need the reduced pressure. 

---

what a load of horse-shit. i'm not going to go to itp tonight, and i'm going to spend the day in bed. i shall make some food, and head out to work tomorrow.

---

why did i even write this? i don't know.
