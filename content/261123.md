lately, i observed that i'm experiencing a higher frequency of social-cocooning than what i did last year. i kept wondering: it must be me. but then, i had a wonderful conversation with [[alua]], [[ryan]], [[antonia]], [[gabriel]] & [[matt]] at the shop-desk on saturday night, and realized maybe not. i think i was trying to be more open to different kinds of people, but now realize that it's exhausting to do so. and new-york forces you do it so much more than bangalore. 

i think i'm happy with a limited set of people that i know i can pleasantly interact with. i don't have to be an asshole to everyone else, but i also don't ~~need~~ want to try as hard.
