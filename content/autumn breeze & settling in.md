pressure; expectations; confusions; desires — balled up into an atomic fission — expanding, over time.

body; tired; finally unsuspended by the weight of a cushioned chair. 

persistent hum of the internal fan. 

body; grazed by an autumn breeze creeping in through the window. 

body; falling deeper into the cushioned chair. 

shoulders; unburdened; falling down like raindrops phoned by gravity. 

breaths, piled up across weeks, slowly escaping; one with the autumn breeze.

atomic fission — finally — out of excitement. 

settling in; exhaling.
