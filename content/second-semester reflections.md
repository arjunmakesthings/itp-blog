### 260416: 
i'm at peace with how the second semester went (although i am currently in the thick of it). 

i learnt so much; became so much better; explored to the breadths of my curiosity. was exhausted; but loved every second of it — this is what i came here to do.

==the best thing that the second semester gave me was the ability to pick something up and figure-it-the-fuck-out.== i did weird circuits, taught myself the serge; managed a 7-ft-tall installation; experimented with molecular-biology with a molecular-biologist; heard many ideas and hypothesized quickly how they must work.

i grew close to the cohort. i'm so thankful for the lovely people who nod in the hallways ([[making others feel seen]]); deal with my excitement; make weird faces; stare; hug; make me laugh; send me texts; respond to the things i make with excitement; entertain; eat food with me; understand; accept. god i love this place.
