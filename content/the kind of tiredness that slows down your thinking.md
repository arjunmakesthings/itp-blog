my body refused to get out of bed today. this is the first 12-hours i went without work since the summer, and i still feel so depleted. 

have you ever been so tired that your brain refuses to think? i experienced this today; perhaps for the first time. a thought came to me, but i was too tired to pursue it. 

the eyes feel like spheres with barren land; body tightened up like a crooked fence; gears of the brain filled with rust; and a feeling of ensuing retirement — i need rest.
