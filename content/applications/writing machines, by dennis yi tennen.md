[dennis](https://english.columbia.edu/content/dennis-yi-tenen) gave a fantastic talk about an alternate history of artificial-intelligence. 

he first argued that <mark>all intelligence is artificial</mark>. we simply use tools & things made by other people. 

for example, i know the correct spelling of "astronomy"; but this is because of the labour of people who built the dictionary, the language, and so on. 

through a tool, i collaborate with other people. 

spoke about <mark>distributed cognition</mark> — there is no sole genius. 

![[z_images/IMG_6775.webp|446x334]]

> i'm entangled in a history of labour. 

there is <mark>shared intellect, not sole intelligence</mark>. 

spoke about generators that existed way before generative-artificial-intelligence.
