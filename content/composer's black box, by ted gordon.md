invited by [[luke dubois]]; held at 2-metrotech. lecture by [[ted gordon]].[[gabriel]] was also there. 

---

traced the history of 5 practitioners experimenting with synthesizers in the 1960s. 

spoke about the idea of a black box: electrical input goes in, a complex non-linear algorithm produces output that cannot be determined; the performance of which cannot be analyzed. 

cybernetics-definitions: weiner defines it as a form of =='self governance'.== an actor gives input to a complex system. 

ra speaks about ==self-discipline as a virtue.== although he claims he was abducted at an early age. 

=='psychedelic technology==' — i will discover myself more through this computational-exploration because it 'flows' through me. 

making sounds from synths is not about the sound, but about ==self-understanding /  a form of sonic exploration== (pauline oliverios), as opposed to subotnick. 

[[ted gordon]] spoke a lot about political commitments and how that shapes the work people do. i wonder: ==whom are my political commitments to?==
