we then spoke about music. i would define it as: 

> intentional organisation of sounds over time (that work for the person who made it).

spoke about fft, tambre (multiple frequencies layered — such as in the human voice), frequency, overtones. mimi explained the western diatonic scale (a relationship between frequencies that sounds good): 

> 1x (of base frequency), 1.125x, 1.25x, 1.33x, 1.5x, 1.66x, 1.875x, 2x.
