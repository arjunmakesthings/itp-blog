while having to articulate & critically engage with [[nature-of-code/index|nature-of-code]], it is apparent to me that ==my practice has always been situated around the idea of 'emergence'. ==

put a few rules together in a 'complex' system, and see what comes out of it. ==through the rules, you understand the system better; or via the rules, you communicate the system better. ==

i think back to the opening line of my master's statement: 

> When I think about the world, I imagine a system — many organisms perpetually interact with one-another, each governed by its own set of rules, collectively unpredictable & complex; with the occasional sprinkle of universal noise. My artistic practice is wholly devoted towards the pursuit of understanding and representing this semi-ordered nature of the world.
