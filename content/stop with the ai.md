i just found out that someone wrote two papers, got them accepted into a premier hci conference, using a large-language-model. they didn't write the paper themselves. the boundaries of human-knowledge, now, pushed forward by the communication of a large-language-model. 

well, no wonder that other students are going to use more l.l.m.s to read l.l.m-written papers. this insane race to publish more; attend more conferences; speak to people dulled out; appeal to brains with less than half a second of attention span — where is the world headed? 

--- 

code-bases; circuits; writing; art; now academic-knowledge — everything going to shit. make more, make infinitely more shit for people to consume, so that everyone gets thinner slices of the pie (but everyone *does* get the pie (more accessible now, no?)). 

this is not the world i wanted to be in. 

something wrong? run to the ai. 

keep making more; like a hamster on a wheel — churn out project after project. 

make extremely messy codebases; write like nothing matters; so that it's impossible for anyone else to pick up from what you started. 

learning? oh, fuck that. just make — you are so empowered to now just be 'creative'. well, for whom?

---

i am fucking done being surrounded by this a.i. bullshit. please keep at it — make your 100 big projects; write your 500 libraries; get famous on your tik-tok & instagram.

i don't want that life. ==i love the work i do — the way i do it. let me be blinded: let me make my 1 small project; write a useless library; and not be a big time artist.==

==i'm going to stop spending so much time being consumed by things that aren't interesting to me. i have enough evidence that i have strong artistic thought; an inkling for poetry; that the work i do adds meaning to my life; and that, despite all the fabulous, big a.i. projects out there,  i still have a tiny audience that wants to listen to me.== 

==the masses are yours.==
