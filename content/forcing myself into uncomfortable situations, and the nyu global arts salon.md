[[mimi]]'s words keep bouncing around in my head. so, i took time out & attended the tisch global arts salon. i saw [[shawn]] there. 

immediately, it was a strange environment — waiters dressed in black, posh food, well-dressed people eating finger-food — i was back to the posh side of the arts. almost ran out, but [[mimi]]'s words wouldn't go away; stayed. 

seated with professors from different tisch departments — art-therapy, musical-theatre-writing & communication-science — and later joined by two graduate students. learnt about them, conversed, was heard. ==we collectively mourned the lack of funding for artistic work; professors and students alike.== 

[[randall]] & i spoke for a little while after, about how people will still produce the work; even with little money.

felt a little bit like the start of every social-innovation-project — give us more money. everything seems to be solved with more money. begs me to wonder — [[if not money, then what?]].
