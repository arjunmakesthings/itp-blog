[[applications/index|applications]] is a required 4-credit class for first-year students, and most of us find ourselves complaining our way through the class. the class, however, has value that reveals itself many weeks later. 

i experienced this myself, and wrote to [[sharon de la cruz]]: 

> dear sharon, 
> 
> over the summer, i wrote [this](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/notes/2026_the-masses-are-yours/page.html). someone over the internet responded to me a few weeks back, and in my reply to them, i found myself recommending finite & infinite games. funnily enough, a year ago, my immediate [reflections](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/itp-blog/applications/finite--and--infinite-games) from the reading read: 
> 
> 	i am so lost in this reading. the author is not convincing, makes unbacked claims, and correlates things based on his own understanding(s) of them.
> 	
> i think i just wanted to say thank you, and that i think applications — for someone absorbent — works in a different timeline than when we're in it. for example, finite & infinite games made sense many months later (and made more sense a whole year later). 
> 
> your paper on curiosity, too, gave me the starting point i was looking for in my [thesis proposal](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/itp-blog/thesis/proposal_261103) (wip). so, thank you. i think the class deserves it; even if you think we're ungrateful in the moment (atleast i felt like i was — a tiny bit; so, sorry). i'm learning.
> 
> best, 
> arjun.
