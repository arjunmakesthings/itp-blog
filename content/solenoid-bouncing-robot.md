after [[efi_class-9]], wanted to use a 555 to send pulses to four solenoids, mounted on a proto-board, to make the proto-board bounce up & down. i'd anticipated the low-bounce, and wanted to use springs to see what'd happen. 

spent time soldering the circuit, but fell short on a few things. 

1) did not account for current, and passed positive-voltage through the transistor, instead of ==connecting both grounds together. ==
2) hot glue with solenoids won't work. solenoids produce a lot of heat, and that melts the glue. need to find a ==better way to mount it. ==
3) probably need to ==test everything first before soldering.== ==everything is a variable== — and i need to make sure everything works (power-supply, number of solenoids, placement, et-cetera).
