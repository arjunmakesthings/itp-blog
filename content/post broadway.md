i had the chance to watch mj on broadway, at the neil simon theatre with [[shikha]] & family. 

broadway was incredible. their performance; the intricacy; the timing; the stage. it felt like the epitome of performing-arts. 

---

i kept looking at the actors — they were really, really good at what they could do. this was far more 'artistic' than any other 'tech-art'; this was so pure; this connected humans with other humans, without the insulation of technology. 

i realised i would never be that good. i would never have that power. i'm not a genius. ==at most, i'm a hard-working mule.==
