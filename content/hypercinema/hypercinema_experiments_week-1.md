worked with [[people/emilia|emilia]] to identify a story-arc and character. she wanted to do horror, i wanted to do more new-york based things. used a mind-map to arrive at common-ground. 

![[z_images/IMG_6164.jpg]]

worked with her to define the story & character. i anticipated team-work to be tricky, and myself to be tricky in team-work, but i think shared excitement conquers differences. 

![[z_images/IMG_6165.jpg]]

we define a story-arc, listed all the sounds we needed. it's evident that we need two kinds of sounds: background, and primary. 

background is best recorded in the natural environment, while the foreground / primary can be recorded artificially. 

---

emilia & i both recorded sounds. i couldn't do so with the zoom recorder; felt awkward. she did, she's more carefree than i am; more confident. 

we then worked together to simulate scenarios (such as running behind her, bumping into her, et-cetera) to record the other sounds.
