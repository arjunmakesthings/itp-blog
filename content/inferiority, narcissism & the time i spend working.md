i've been in consistent therapy for 4 months now. today, [[nausheen]] & i dived into my tendency to feel superior at times; something that also came up in my [[conversation with tanika williams]], and felt in an earlier conversation with [[people/prachi|prachi]] (and many others). 

---
i realise that i'm jealous of my peers, for being able to do other things apart from work, and that stems from the inferiority that i have in comparison. i am surrounded by smarter people, more original thinkers, and more experienced professionals. 

time is the only thing in my control, and i'm using every bit of it to work — in an attempt to keep up. maybe this is why it feels like [[there's too little time.]]

i need to balance this before i crash, and give myself the <mark>permission to enjoy</mark> once in a while; something that came up in my [[conversation with dan-o-sullivan]] too. 

she also asked me to <mark>look for the good in people, beyond the work they produce</mark>. we also realised that i don't know how to make friends, or enjoy; something that [[people/sakina|sakina]] & [[people/shobhan|shobhan]] (along with others) had previously expressed. 

i also must make <mark>time to rest</mark>, because <mark>rest is as important as work</mark> . perhaps this is what the kind-hearted folk at itp mean, when they ask me to go home, or remind me that it's just been two weeks (and i have two more years of this).
