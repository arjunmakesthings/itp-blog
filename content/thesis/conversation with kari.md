had a great conversation with [[kari love]]. 

- she disagreed with [[itp theses traps]], and told me cases where many might be more in service of the project than one. resonated with my initial disagreement too.
- connections between ==braille as a tactile interface & haptics== (distance between things makes it open for perception). 
- she also spoke about how cellular automata is prevalent even in human biology; like so:
	- ![[limb-ca.webp|385]]

- she asked me to look at what the core of my thesis is: is it ==perception or materiality?== either is okay, but i need to know. she mentioned how [[luisa pereira]] told her once to ==keep asking why-s== (which was amusing to me because i tell students the same, but failed to follow my own advice).  ==why cellular automata -> why that -> why that.== ==if i'm using water, what about that material excites me? why water?==
- mentioned that it might be worthwhile to speak with [[tom-igoe]] & [[daniel shiffman]], because of their interests in 'making computation physical' and 'nature & computation'. 
- we spoke about her work often stems from the ==curiosity of a material: what can a material be that it hasn't been yet?==
- we also spoke about ==making people be cells and acting out cellular automata rules== — what would that look like? want to speak with [[bairui]] & do it as a performance piece. 
- ==find something interesting, and the thesis is a chance to go deeper into the thing.==

speaking to her also made me realize how clear she is as an artist, and how developed her choices are. she's — as [[prachi]] would have called it — 'mulled' over things (and wandered) greatly.
