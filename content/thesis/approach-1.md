the merit of this thesis is to prototype & move fast. [[electrowetting]] took up a lot of my time — i still think it's interesting, but at a certain point i need to prototype with other things. 

what i need to explore quickly are (for the lack of a better word) modalities — i.e: how can a cell express its state?

i need to explore one modality at any given point in time. for example:

- movement:
	- x, y dimension
	- z dimension
- sound
- warmth
- rotation
- size (inflatable)

i think it would be poetic if you can connect human behavior to cell states; i.e how do we express? 

- movement
	- x, y dimension
	- z dimension
- sound:
	- loudness
	- frequency
	- rate
- density:
	- softness / hardness (to touch)
- reaction (goes in line with [[danny-rozin]] saying: what happens if i can influence the behavior of the automata midway): 
	- to touch

i couldn't do movement with electrowetting. so what — what else can i do?
