had a beautiful chat with [[nasif]]. 

we first discussed the differences between my original premise & my experiments — i wanted to make people experience cellular-automata with senses other than sight; yet my experiments were all around sight. 

i thought about why that was — i am intuitively drawn more towards sight; but i also recognize that it is safer than other territories. who wants to say "i'm going to make people taste cellular-automata"? realized that i was being avoidant; that i had fears; that i was playing too safe. 

he also probed further into the project being an art piece versus a tool. is it automata in different medium (perhaps as creative research) or is it a tool that other people will use? he explained his disdain for the overuse of the word 'tool'; which echoes my feelings of itp-projects being one-offs — even though they're termed as 'tools'. 

he didn't see value in it as a tool; i disagreed. maybe he's right though, and i wouldn't know yet. 

we made a ==connection between zimoun sculptures== — [[work, by zimoun]] — and physical-expression of rule0based systems. i hadn't seen that yet. 

he ended by explaining a series of 'itp-theses-traps'; one of which was to make a series (an alumni mentioned this to him). so, we discussed ==whether i want to make many or make one, well-executed one.== this is largely because an itp-thesis must end in a commitment; because it is easy to hide behind experiments & not make decisions. related to [[collages, not committing, and making many]].
