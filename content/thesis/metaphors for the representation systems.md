expansion on metaphor for [[electrowetting]]: 

us:

drops of water, 
over a high-resolution grid. 

attracted & repelled, 
by forces embedded. 

part of a system,
we can't comprehend.

our very beings:
splitting,
merging,
moving,
shattering,
and becoming whole;

all of this going on at the individual,
indecipherable as a whole.


---

the experience of the self
versus others 
and my relationship with everyone else



---

for [[communication expression]]: 

just words right now:

speak
or listen

never together; 
always only one.

your words
affect
my response
and so does mine
to yours 

and on and on we go. 

unending 
cyclical
