we live in a world mediated by technology. i am surrounded by computers — tiny & giant.

brett victor argues that ==a good tool amplifies human capabilities== ([[a brief rant on the future of interaction design, by bret victor]]). however, tools today are rarely doing so; because they've been made with the intention of profiting someone else.

the thesis is an attempt to break away from mass-produced, centrally controlled big-tech, and to make hyperspecific, localised, self-owned & hosted alternatives.
