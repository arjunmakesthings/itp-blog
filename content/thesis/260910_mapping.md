figured out that there's no anchor in the project. decided to set restrictions, and followed up from [[conversation with danny-rozin]]. some ideas: 

- out of all the senses, i'm most excited by ==sight.== some senses are inaccessible as an interface-input. 
- ==a cell can be defined as an autonomous unit that acts according to its neighbors.== a cell keeps track of the following: 
	- neighborhood
	- state
- a controller on top can keep track of all cells, ids, and load a program onto the cell. this can be the controller that sends the program onto the cell. 
- the ==perceivable expression of the cell is by its degrees of freedom==. the more degrees of freedom that the system can afford, the more possibilities there are for the expression of an automata. 
- the enquiry is moving towards the following: can a pixel be autonomous? therefore, collective-behavior is complex & unpredictable; is emergent; dependent on seed & noise in the real world (isn't perfect anymore; like the screen). 

for expressive work, the automaton needs to be set free.
