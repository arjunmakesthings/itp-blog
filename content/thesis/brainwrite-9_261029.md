i need to be able to explain to a person ==what i'm doing; why i'm doing it; and why they might care==, and the way i do it must be ==convincing==.

hence, this proposal:

---

**basic questions:**

**what are you trying to do:**

i am trying to make alternative display mechanisms for people to sense two dimensional automata. 

**why are you doing this:** 

the field of cellular automata 

i think the field of cellular automata has largely been studied as an exercise of mathematics or discovery (see https://labofbabel.org/home). however, there is expressive power in cellular automata — i wish to prove that. this is the software part of my project. 

why physical: 

i think the ability to visually represent the output of an algorithm will always be present — a computer screen. i wonder what happens when this is brought out; brought out into a world where the physical forces of the world affect it — when the system is designed not by an engineer, but an expressive artist. 

**why should other people care:** 

so much of our time is spent in thinking about why something happened, or how it happened, or what happens. 

the world around you seems complex, but it isn't — this is the study of complex systems. 

i will demonstrate how one can attempt to understand & express what something feels like, in the language of encoding it into a rule-based system. 

what does that allow people to do?

it helps you fit it into a rational model, and maybe, just maybe, arrive at a closer version of the truth. 

**what do you want people to take out of this:** 

i want people to see that the world around them is not so mysterious. they can also spend time in understanding the world around them.

so much of our time is spent in acts of information-seeking: why something is the way it is; how something works; what something feels like; who someone is. whether explorative or exploitative, our lives are spent exercising curiosity. 

with that, i invite you to the study of complex systems.
