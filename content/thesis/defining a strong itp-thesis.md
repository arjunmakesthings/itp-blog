> A strong ITP thesis:
> 
> - articulates your interests, builds on your strengths, deepens them
>   
>   demonstrates what you have learned during your time at ITP
>   
>   explores the creative possibilities and cultural and societal effects of technology and interactivity
>   
>   demonstrates that you have engaged with the context in which your work exists by doing research, conducting interviews, seeking feedback from field experts and from your intended audience
>   
>   includes a prototype/artifact (or a series of them) that incorporate these findings
