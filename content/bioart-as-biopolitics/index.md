# why: 
biological-material as working material for me as an artist has always been on my radar, but i never had the tools or education to formally engage with it. 

over the years, i've stumbled upon the following (and grown curious): 
- book on dna-sequencing, and the fact that i am a certain kind of human is dictated because of certain letters in my dna. 
- thejesh making his dna open-source in 2014: https://github.com/thejeshgn/dna
- 'de-extinction' work by colossal: https://colossal.com
- the term 'biohacking'

the course also mentions biopolitics, and with heather's background in academia, i expect the class to give me (as [[mimi]] called it) enough thinking material for my plate.
