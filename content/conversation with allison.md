i spoke to [[people/allison parrish|allison parrish]] about my enquiry with [living messages](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2023-24_living-messages/page.html). 

we spoke about how the <mark>letters were simply standing in for shapes</mark> — i could've used shapes to communicate the same feeling — and then discussed the validity of the notion that text may be used as an image.

she also made me realise that <mark>it's okay for a project to die</mark>, and that <mark>you may not find many people who share the same curiosity as you</mark>.

---
