presented my [living-messages](https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2023-24_living-messages/page.html) enquiry at the feedback collective. 

[[people/jasmine|jasmine]], [[pedro]], [[people/antonia|antonia]] and another person whose name i can't recollect were there. 

---
[[people/jasmine|jasmine]] & [[people/pedro|pedro]] suggested that i might consider <mark>juxtaposition</mark> to make the outcomes more interesting — and to <mark>leave room for interpretation</mark>. 

they also probed me to consider where i want this enquiry to go: is it a <mark>body of work</mark>, it is supposed to <mark>culminate in something grand</mark>, or are they <mark>experiments to see how far i can go</mark>?

the person whose name i can't recollect also suggested that i might reconsider what 'living' meant — like [[people/jasmine|jasmine]] said, <mark>text on a screen also lives</.mark>, and text on stone lives beyond death. 

[[people/pedro|pedro]] also mentioned that any area that i go — exploration, culmination, et-cetera — is okay, but i <mark>must decide that (where i want this to go)</mark>.
