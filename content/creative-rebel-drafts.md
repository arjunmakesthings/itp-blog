meaningful: 
instead of a project (or multiple projects), my central enquiry is the most meaningful to me — projects are mere ways to grapple with it.

as i grew up to become a creative-professional, i fell in love with the idea of employing logic as a creative medium. when people asked me about how i saw the world, i would tell them that i imagine a system — many organisms perpetually interacting with one-another; each governed by their own sets of rules, collectively unpredictable & complex; with the occasional sprinkle of universal noise. over time, my artistic practice became wholly devoted towards the pursuit of understanding & representing this semi-ordered nature of the world.

i believe all of this solidified during my undergraduate thesis — where i programmed a web-game (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2022_in-between-life-and-death/page.html) that allowed players to progress through 'life', governed by an algorithm that was based on maslow's hierarchy of human-needs. there was something about the logical simplicity of expressing something so commonly thought of as complex — human-existence — that drew me closer to the idea of using logic as an expressive medium.  

in the past, i have made logic-systems that replicate abstract-expressionistic paintings (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2023-24_digital-painters/page.html), systems that allow me to communicate hidden feelings while writing text (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2023-24_living-messages/page.html), what it feels like to exist in this world (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2024_existence/page.html), and the infinite beauty of something so elementary like the number line (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2024_drawings-on-a-cartesian-plane/page.html).

about: 

i grew up all across india, moving with / between my parents, and studying in seven different schools. i was athletic & curious, and aspired to become a professional athlete.

when that didn't pan out, i pivoted to design, and studied communication-design at iiad, delhi. my professors moved me, and i became naively inspired by the idea of using design to change the world. work also introduced me to the artistic potential of programming, and i used it as a gateway into interdisciplinary work. i worked with science-gallery-bengaluru in my final year — programming a web-game (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2022_in-between-life-and-death/page.html) and exploring citizen-science (https://arjunmakesthings.github.io/projects/2022_earth-2052/page.html).

after graduating, i worked as a design-researcher for 2+ years — at tinkerlabs and treemouse — in their social-impact practices, enabling better access & adoption to services in the lowest-resource regions of india. in addition to my day-job, i continued my computational enquiries at night. after two years, disillusioned by the industry, i resigned to grow my own practice, and did design-research, product-design, creative-coding, and taught design in undergraduate & postgraduate programs across india. 

over time, i became exhausted with the idea of living a split-life — making things for other people (design-work), and making things to satisfy my own curiosity (computational-practice). so, in 2025, i moved to new york to study at the interactive-telecommunications-programme at new-york-univeristy on a near-full scholarship; allowing me the time & space to engage solely with my curiosity for two full years. i hope to leave the program with an economically-feasible space to continue engaging with my curiosity, and mentoring young-students in computational-expression.
