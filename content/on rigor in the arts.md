i often think about rigor (such as in [[the lack of rigor in creatives]]). [[shloka]] recently mentioned that they thought that rigor meant hard-work. 

so,  here is a clarifying note about what i mean by rigor, in the sub-context of the arts:

the american-english-dictionary defines rigor as: 

> the quality of being extremely thorough, exhaustive, or accurate: his analysis is lacking in rigor.

rigor encompasses not only hard-work; but discipline (the [swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Style_(design)) are stellar examples of this), organization (of files, wires, et-cetera), polish (craftsmanship), adherence to strict guidelines (like [[10 bullets. by tom sachs]]); an almost militaristic-pursuit of top-notch quality. 

good-enough is not good enough. 

that is rigor in the arts.
