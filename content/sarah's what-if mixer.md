related to [[intro-to-physical-computing/is it is our duty to make people notice things?|is it is our duty to make people notice things?]]:  [[sarah rothberg]]'s work invited people to speculative-scenarios, and made them converse with each other. 

after 8-minutes of people awkwardly walking around the room, most amidst known circles, everyone was called to a common spot and then forced to talk to a new person. i spoke with someone much older than myself, and we had an interesting conversation about what the world *might* look like, if things on our card were true. 

it's nice to *force* an experience onto someone — someone who's willing to be part of an experience, of-course.
