[[tom]] had introduced this book to me. i keep this collection of quotes from the physical copy that i borrowed from him. 

the book is about work done by two people in this studio: https://www.dailytouslesjours.com/en

they make social-experiences, often between strangers; in the public infrastructure of a city. 

> ==singing in harmony with others== — whether in a stadium, around a campfire, or in a choir — is the ==experience of a common emotion.== beyond beautiful voices, genuine pleasure comes from singing together. 

> ==choirs are the only place where people come together and express a common emotion.==

> ==music demands a much lighter cognitive load from its audience than having to pay attention to a sequence of images.==

> ==one can be moved by music while still being aware of what is going on around them.==

> the happiest countries in the world also have the highest levels of interpersonal trust. 

from the preface — strangers need strange moments together.
